# Custom theme contract

A custom theme is a visual skin, not a layout preset.

Allowed: colors, typography, surfaces, borders, shadows, backgrounds, filters, gradients, animations and visual effects.

Forbidden: changing routing, component hierarchy, channel/profile layout, `channel_style`, `channel_info_layout`, or any user-selected structural preference.

Cosmic Panda follows the YouTube 2012 visual direction but remains a custom interpretation with additional depth, glow and atmospheric effects.
